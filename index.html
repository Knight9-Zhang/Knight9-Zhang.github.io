About Me

Background & Education

I am YiChi Zhang (张亦弛), a graduate researcher and developer currently pursuing a Master's degree at China Agricultural University, where I focus on the intersection of animal genetics, breeding, and computational biology.

My research centers on leveraging bioinformatics approaches to unravel the genomic architecture underlying complex traits in livestock and aquatic species. I am passionate about developing computational pipelines that bridge the gap between raw sequencing data and meaningful biological insights.

Beyond the lab, I enjoy exploring open-source tooling and building software that makes scientific workflows more reproducible and accessible.
